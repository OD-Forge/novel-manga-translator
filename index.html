Features

Made for people who read a lot

Live screen

Translate what's on screen

Draw a box over any app or browser and the translation appears on top, once or continuously. Works for novels and manga, with hotkeys even while the app is minimized.

YOUR SCREEN魔導書は、まだ眠っている。
→
OVERLAYThe grimoire is still asleep.
Reader

Read it your way

Original, translation or bilingual. Dark, Sepia or Light, and it remembers the exact paragraph you stopped at.

Editor

Polish every line

Quality checks flag missing terms, numbers, quotes and untranslated lines. Every change keeps its history.

Compare

Pick the best version

Run one paragraph through up to three engines side by side and keep the one you like.

History & queue

Line up a whole series

Queue volumes and close the app. The queue picks up where it left off next time.

Story Memory

Names that stay names PRO

Characters, places and terms stay consistent from chapter 1 to chapter 400. Local AI can scan the book and suggest them for you.

葵 → Aoi王都 → Royal Capital魔導書 → Grimoire
Export

Take it anywhere PRO

Novels as PDF, EPUB, Word, text or web pages. Manga as PDF, CBZ or images.
