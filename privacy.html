Privacy

Your books stay yours

Novel & Manga Translator is built to work on your own computer. There is no account, no cloud library and no analytics. This page explains exactly what stays on your PC and what leaves it, and only when you choose.

01

What stays on your PC

Everything you import and create lives in the app's data folder on your computer: %LOCALAPPDATA%\NovelMangaTranslator. We have no server that receives it.

Your library

Novels, manga pages, covers, chapters, reading position, bookmarks and collections.

On this PC

Translations and edits

Machine translations, your edits, revision history, Story Memory names and terms.

On this PC

Local AI and text recognition

The translation model and the OCR models come inside the app and run on your processor. Text translated with Local AI never leaves your computer.

Offline

Logs

Technical logs for troubleshooting stay in the data folder. They are never uploaded. "Export system info" copies version and engine status to your clipboard only when you press it, without book text or keys.

Never sent
02

When text goes online

Text leaves your PC only when you pick an online translation engine. Then only the paragraphs being translated (with DeepL, also the paragraph before as unbilled context) are sent directly from your PC to that provider. They do not pass through any server of ours. Your files, covers and library are never sent.

Google Translate

Free online engine. Text is sent to Google's public translation service.

Online · Free

DeepL, Google Cloud, Microsoft Translator

Sent with your own API key to your own account with that provider. If Story Memory is on, approved terms are kept as a glossary in your DeepL account, and you can remove them from Settings.

Pro

Each provider handles that text under its own privacy policy. Check their terms if you translate private material online.

2 · The app asksThe first time a book would go online, unless you turned this off.
3 · Only text is sentParagraph by paragraph, straight to the provider.
03

Your controls

Find these in Settings › Privacy.

Local-only mode

Blocks every online engine everywhere in the app: the queue, the editor, comparisons and live screen. Nothing you read can be sent online.

Off by default

Ask before sending text online

Before a book's text goes to an online engine for the first time, the app asks you. Your answer is remembered for that book.

On by default
04

Live screen translation

Live screen only captures the box you draw, and only while a session is running. Captures are read in memory and are not saved as files. The app's own overlays are excluded from capture. The list of recent live translations keeps the last 50 in memory and is cleared when you close the app. Apps that block screenshots stay blocked.

05

API keys

Keys for DeepL, Google Cloud and Microsoft Translator are stored in Windows Credential Manager on your PC. If it is not available, they are kept in a separate file inside your data folder. Keys are only ever sent to the provider they belong to.

06

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase handled by the Microsoft Store. We never see your payment details. The app asks the Store whether your Microsoft account owns Pro and keeps that status on your PC.

07

Links and updates

The app only opens links to the Microsoft Store, YouTube (for how-to videos) and our own site. Updates come through the Microsoft Store.

08

Backup and deletion

To back up everything, copy the data folder while the app is closed. To delete everything, uninstall the app and remove the data folder. Because nothing is stored on our side, there is nothing for us to delete.
